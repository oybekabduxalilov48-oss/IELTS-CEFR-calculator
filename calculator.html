Understanding IELTS Scores

Band Score System

IELTS uses a 9-band scoring system. Each band corresponds to a specific level of English proficiency.

Overall Band Score

Your overall band score is the average of your four section scores, rounded to the nearest half band.

Academic vs General

Reading and Writing sections differ between Academic and General Training modules.

Understanding CEFR Levels

Six-Level Scale

CEFR defines language proficiency at six levels: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and C2.

International Standard

Used worldwide to describe language ability in reading, writing, listening and speaking.

35-Question Format

Official CEFR assessments use 35 questions per section for accurate level determination.
